**🚀 Project** · step 5 of 5 · [Twelve stars](#expedice-program-en)

Invent an Advent-style puzzle for Tomáš or for anyone from the club.

1. **Story and data.** A short story (sledges, a bakery, a present warehouse) and input as a list of numbers or lines of text. Make the data with a program, so that there are at least 50 of them.
2. **Two parts.** Part 1 can be solved with one loop and a condition. Part 2 needs the program to remember something: the previous value, a record, a counter.
3. **Example.** A small example, say five values, with the correct answer. It lets the solver check whether they understood the puzzle.
4. **Your solution.** Write a program that solves the puzzle, and check it on the example.
5. **Traps and edges.** Add at least one edge case to the data: a zero, a negative number, two equal values in a row. The puzzle must make clear what counts in such a case.
6. **Versions.** Save each working version with a message following your rules from the analysis: in Git, or at least as `puzzle-v1.py`, `puzzle-v2.py` with a note on what changed.
7. **Testing on a person.** Give the puzzle to someone to solve. Where did the solver get stuck? Was it the puzzle, or unclear instructions? Fix the instructions.

You’re done when someone solves the puzzle using only your instructions, with no extra explaining, and their answer matches yours.
