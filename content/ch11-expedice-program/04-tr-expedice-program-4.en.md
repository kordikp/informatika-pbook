**👥 Crew** · step 4 of 5 · [Twelve stars](#expedice-program-en)

[Advent of Code](https://adventofcode.com/) is free, in English, and since 2025 it has **12 puzzles**, one every day from 1 December. You can solve them in any language, even in blocks. Each puzzle has two parts, and for each one you get your own input data.

**Account:** you can only log in through other services, such as GitHub or Reddit. Those are for ages 13 and up. If you’re not 13 yet, ask a parent to log in with their account, or solve only the **example** in the text of each puzzle. You can read it without logging in, and the correct answer is given with it.

**How to do it:** read the whole puzzle. Solve the example by hand on paper. Write a program and run it on the example first. Only when it’s right, run it on your own data.

**Leaderboard:** there is no longer a global leaderboard, but a parent or the club leader can set up a private one for your crew. Your pace isn’t a race; the puzzles stay on the website after Christmas too.

**3 rules for taking part safely:**
1. Don’t put your full name, school or photo in your profile or your solutions. A nickname is enough.
2. The author asks you not to solve the puzzles with AI. They are made for people, and otherwise you won’t learn anything.
3. Don’t copy or publish the puzzles or the input data anywhere; they belong to the author. You may share your own code, and who with is something to agree with a parent.

> **Stay safe:** before you join a crew, read the page [Safe in communities](#bezpecne-v-komunitach-en). Set up your account with a parent and never give your address, school or phone number.
