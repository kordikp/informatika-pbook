**🛠️ Side trip** · [The case of the late bus](#expedice-data-en)

The state and towns publish thousands of sets of **open data**: data that anyone may use, and it is clear how. Open the [National Open Data Catalogue](https://data.gov.cz/) and find three datasets about your town or region (transport, schools, budget, air quality). For each one, find out the format (CSV is best), who publishes the data, how often they are updated and what is missing from them. Then download the population of your town by age from the [Public Database of the Czech Statistical Office](https://csu.gov.cz/verejna-databaze) and answer with a graph: is my town getting older? Compare it with the whole region. Watch out when comparing a small village with a big city: compare **shares** (percentages), not numbers.
