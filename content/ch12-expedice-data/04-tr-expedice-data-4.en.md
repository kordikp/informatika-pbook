**👥 Crew** · step 4 of 5 · [The case of the late bus](#expedice-data-en)

**The ISLP competition** (International Statistical Literacy Project) is an international statistical poster competition for school pupils. For the 2026–2027 round, the rules say:
- categories **9–12 years**, **13–15 years** and **16–18 years**;
- a team has **2 to 5** pupils, a poster by one person is not accepted;
- you choose the topic yourselves, and taking part is **free**;
- you enter through the **national round**, usually through your school and a teacher. Deadlines differ by country, the international deadline is 14 March 2027;
- the best posters will be shown at the World Statistics Congress in Lusaka, Zambia, in July 2027.

The details are on the [competition page](https://iase-web.org/islp-poster-competition-2026-2027) (in English). Show it to your maths or computing teacher and ask whether they will help the school with the entry.

**Zooniverse** is the biggest citizen science community: volunteers help scientists sort photos of animals, galaxies or old manuscripts. You can try a few tasks straight away. If you are under 16, a parent must approve your registration. [How to get involved](https://www.zooniverse.org/get-involved) (in English).

**Three rules for taking part safely:**
1. **An adult sets up entries and accounts.** Your school enters you for the competition, and you set up a Zooniverse account only with a parent. Never get round the age limit.
2. **Data about people are anonymous.** Names, photos, addresses or the school of the people you asked don’t belong on the poster or in a forum. When you run a survey, tell people what you will use the data for.
3. **In forums you talk about data, not about yourself.** No real name, town or contact details. If someone messages you privately about something off topic, don’t reply and tell an adult.

> **Stay safe:** before you join a crew, read the page [Safe in communities](#bezpecne-v-komunitach-en). Set up your account with a parent and never give your address, school or phone number.
