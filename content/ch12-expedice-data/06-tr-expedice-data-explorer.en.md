**👀 Side trip** · [The case of the late bus](#expedice-data-en)

Scientists have millions of photos from camera traps and nobody can get through them all. On [Zooniverse](https://www.zooniverse.org/get-involved) (in English, an account under 16 only with a parent’s approval), volunteers sort them. Choose one project and record 30 classifications. Then think about it: each photo is usually labelled by several people. Why? What if three people agree and two don’t? Design a rule that picks the final answer from five answers, and find a case where it fails. This is how **labelled data** are made, which AI then learns from. If the labels are wrong, the model learns wrong too. This continues in the chapter on artificial intelligence.
