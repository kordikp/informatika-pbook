**🛠 Workshop** · step 2 of 5 · [The case of the late bus](#expedice-data-en)

**CODAP** is a free web app for exploring data, made for school pupils. It needs no account and no installation. The interface is in English, and a few words are enough: *table*, *graph*, *attribute* (a column).

1. **Data (5 min).** Copy Ema’s table into a spreadsheet and save it as **CSV** (comma-separated values). Name the columns without accents: day, delay, weather.
2. **Opening (3 min).** Open [codap.concord.org](https://codap.concord.org/), start the app and drag the CSV file into the window. A table appears.
3. **First graph (5 min).** Create a graph and drag the column *delay* onto the horizontal axis. Each journey is one dot. You’ll spot day 7 straight away: a dot far to the right.
4. **Groups (5 min).** Drag the column *weather* onto the vertical axis. The dots split into two rows: dry and rain.
5. **Measuring (5 min).** In the graph menu (the ruler icon), switch on the mean (*mean*) and the median (*median*). Compare them with your calculation from the first step. Do they match?
6. **Experiment (5 min).** Delete day 7 in the table and watch how the mean lines move.
7. **Saving (2 min).** Save the document to your computer.

**Done when:** you have a graph with two groups and you can see the mean and the median for each. Bonus: add a column *weekday* and find out whether Monday is worse than rain.
