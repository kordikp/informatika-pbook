**🚀 Project** · step 5 of 5 · [The case of the late bus](#expedice-data-en)

**1. Question (10 min).** Choose a question you can answer with data within two weeks. It must be measurable. Examples: “Is the morning queue in the school canteen longer on Mondays?”, “How many minutes a day do I spend on my phone on weekdays and at the weekend?” (data from the phone, with a parent’s consent), “How has the population of my town or village changed over 30 years?” (data from the [Public Database of the Czech Statistical Office](https://csu.gov.cz/verejna-databaze) or from the [National Open Data Catalogue](https://data.gov.cz/)).

**2. Table and rules (15 min).** Design the columns, value types and rules for writing them, as in the first step. Write down in advance how many records you want (at least 20).

**3. Collecting and cleaning.** Collect the data. Then find and fix: typos, different formats, duplicates, missing values. For each fix, note down what and why.

**4. Analysis (CODAP).** Group the data, compare the mean and the median, look for outliers and third things.

**5. Graph (Datawrapper).** On [Datawrapper](https://www.datawrapper.de/) you can create a graph without registering. Paste in the data, choose the type of graph to fit the question (change over time = line, comparison = bars), add axis labels and the data source, and download a PNG.

**6. Explanation.** Three sentences: what the data show, what they don’t show and what could distort the result.

**Check:** go through your poster with the “honest graph vs. trick” table from the analysis. Bar axis starting at zero? Source given? No personal data?
