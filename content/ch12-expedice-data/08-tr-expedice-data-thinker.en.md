**🔬 Side trip** · [The case of the late bus](#expedice-data-en)

A table with thousands of rows and several linked tables is hard to handle in a spreadsheet. That is why **databases** and the language **SQL** exist. Play [SQL Murder Mystery](https://mystery.knightlab.com/) (in English): a murder has happened in SQL City, and the only clue is a database of police reports, driving licences and gym visits. Start with the [walkthrough for beginners](https://mystery.knightlab.com/walkthrough.html). You’ll learn three commands that are enough for most of the work: **SELECT** (what I want to see), **WHERE** (a filter) and **JOIN** (joining tables through a key, exactly as in the challenge “Two Emas and one key”). Once you find the culprit, write your own mini database: the tables Pupils, Books and Loans, and a query that finds all overdue books.
