**🚀 Project** · step 5 of 5 · [The weekend a game was made](#expedice-hry-en)

Now you’ll build your own game on the theme “Everything speeds up”. Choose your tool: Scratch, [MakeCode Arcade](https://arcade.makecode.com) or Godot from the workshop.

**Jam rule: one mechanic, one screen, done.** A small game that can be finished is better than a big one that won’t start.

**Required parts:**
- **Game loop with dt.** Movement is `speed × dt` and `dt` is limited so that a freeze doesn’t break the game.
- **Collisions with tests.** Write three tests in advance (input and expected output): an ordinary one, touching at the edge and the fastest object in the game. Calculate whether the fastest object can’t fly through.
- **State machine.** At least three states of the character or an enemy. Draw a table of states and events and check that it has no empty cell.
- **Seed.** If the game uses randomness, show the seed on the screen. Whoever finds a bug reports the number to you.
- **Difficulty curve.** The game speeds up, but the first minute can be managed on the first try.

**Schedule:**
1. By 10:00: a playable prototype with grey rectangles, no graphics.
2. By 15:00: all required parts.
3. 15:00–17:00: give the game to three people. Don’t explain anything, just watch and note where they got stuck.
4. By 19:00: fix the three worst places, then graphics and sound.
5. By 20:00: publish the game under a nickname, with a parent, and write a short description of the controls.

**Success criterion:** someone who has never seen the game finishes it, and you have a list of what you’ll fix next time.
