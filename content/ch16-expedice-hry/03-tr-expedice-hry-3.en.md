**👥 Crew** · step 3 of 5 · [The weekend a game was made](#expedice-hry-en)

A **game jam** is a competition in which a game on a set theme is made in a few days. It’s not about winning but about finishing the game. Here are three ways in, by age:

| Where | Age | How to join |
|---|---|---|
| [A studio on Scratch](https://scratch.mit.edu) | all ages, younger ones enter a parent’s e-mail | Create a studio, announce a theme and a deadline, invite friends. |
| [MakeCode Arcade jams](https://arcade.makecode.com/gamejam/all) | creating with no limits, forum account with a parent | Look through past themes, submit with a parent. |
| [GGJ Next](https://itch.io/jam/ggjnext2026) | ages 12–17 | Best through a school or club whose site a teacher registers. Virtually through itch.io, the account is managed by a parent or teacher. |

GGJ Next runs roughly from November to March and the 2026 edition has already finished, so watch for the next one to be announced. Mostly secondary-school students go to [Czech jams](https://itch.io/jam/ostravsky-game-jam-2025). Look through their games for inspiration.

Services like Discord or GitHub have an age limit of 13. Younger people read them without an account, or use them with a parent. Never change your age.

**Three safety rules:**
1. **A nickname, not you.** Your profile and the game’s title must not contain your name, school, age or photo.
2. **Official channels only.** Talk on the jam page and with your team. Anyone who invites you to another app, a call or a private message is a reason to stop replying and tell an adult.
3. **Download nothing and pay for nothing.** Don’t open files, “helper programs” or links from strangers. The jam is free.

> **Stay safe:** before you join a crew, read the page [Safe in communities](#bezpecne-v-komunitach-en). Set up your account with a parent and never give your address, school or phone number.
