**🛠 Workshop** · step 2 of 5 · [The bird feeder model](#expedice-ai-en)

The birds outside the window won’t wait, so start with things on your desk. Use [Teachable Machine](https://teachablemachine.withgoogle.com/) (free, no account, in English).

1. **Get Started → Image Project → Standard image model.**
2. Create three classes, for example *mug*, *pencil case*, *nothing*. The *nothing* class is an empty desk. Without it the model always has to guess something.
3. Record at least **40 images** from the camera for each class. Turn the object, change the distance, switch the lamp on and off.
4. Click **Train Model** and wait.
5. **Test on new data:** take the laptop to another room or use a different mug. Do 10 tries for each class and write them into the confusion matrix from the previous step.
6. **Break the model on purpose:** train a new version where you photograph the pencil case only on a white desk. Then put it on a dark cloth. What happens? Write it down. That’s bias, and you’ve just made it with your own hands.

**Stay safe:** photograph things, not classmates. Another person’s face only with their consent. Don’t save the model to Google Drive without a parent.

Got a micro:bit? Try the same with movements in [micro:bit CreateAI](https://createai.microbit.org/): waving, clapping, keeping still.
