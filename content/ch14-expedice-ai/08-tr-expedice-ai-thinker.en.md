**🔬 Side trip** · [The bird feeder model](#expedice-ai-en)

Take your three-sentence model from the generative model challenge and compare it with a real one. In [Transformer Explainer](https://github.com/poloclub/transformer-explainer) (in English, demo in the project description), type the start of an English sentence, for example “The capital of Austria is”. Find which tokens the model offers and with what probability. Then try a sentence the model can’t know, for example about the river Mlýnka. Does it offer a confident answer? Write down three sentences: one where the model is right, one where it hallucinates, and one where it is unsure (the probabilities are close together). Explain how the three situations differ.
