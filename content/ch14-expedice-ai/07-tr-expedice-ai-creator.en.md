**🛠️ Side trip** · [The bird feeder model](#expedice-ai-en)

Test the model from the workshop **by groups**. Split the test shots into groups: daylight and lamp, light and dark background, close and far. Work out the accuracy for each group separately. Find the group where the model is worst, and improve it only by changing the training data. Criterion: the gap between the best and the worst group shrinks to at least half. Then go back to the catch from the world of creating with AI: an image generator draws “doctors” as men. Write what its team would have to do to change that, and why you can’t simply “ban” it.
