**🚀 Project** · step 5 of 5 · [An appathon in one weekend](#expedice-aplikace-en)

Build a second version of the app for a group you know: a scout troop, a club, a band, a sports team. Stick to these milestones:

1. **One sentence for the problem.** “The troop doesn’t know who paid for what and who is bringing the tents.” If you can’t say it in one sentence, the app will do everything and nothing properly.
2. **Three screens, no more.** Draw them on paper. On each one, circle one main button and put it where your thumb can reach.
3. **A data table.** Which columns, which are required, in what format? Save expenses, not debts, and give every record a unique ID.
4. **A state diagram.** What states does the app have, for example “no internet”? What does each button do in each state?
5. **Roles.** Who is allowed to delete what? Does the leader see everything and a member only their own payments? Which phone permissions does the app not need?
6. **First round of testing with five people.** Give them a task, stay quiet and note down every hesitation. Fix the three worst problems.
7. **Second round with five different people.** Did it get better?

Measure success with numbers: how many testers out of five manage the main task without help, and in how many seconds. Goal: at least 4 out of 5 within 30 seconds.

If AI writes part of the code for you, have it explain every block and write yourself a test for the edge cases: an empty amount, a double tap, a lost signal. You are responsible for the app, not the AI.
