**🛠️ Side trip** · [An appathon in one weekend](#expedice-aplikace-en)

Blocks have a ceiling: more complicated calculations get lost in them. The next step is [App Lab from Code.org](https://code.org/applab). You design screens in Design Mode, save data in Data Mode, and with one tap the blocks switch to real JavaScript. Work through the [intro course](https://studio.code.org/s/applab-intro) (in English) and rewrite your expense counter from Saturday’s workshop in App Lab. Then try writing the debt settling from the challenge “Fewest transfers” as a function in text, without blocks. **Criterion:** for the balances from the challenge, the function returns four transfers and their total matches. Do you have an iPad or a Mac at home? The Swift Playgrounds app will teach you the Swift language, which iPhone apps are made in, and from age 15 you can enter the Swift Student Challenge with it.
