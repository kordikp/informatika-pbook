**🔬 Side trip** · [An appathon in one weekend](#expedice-aplikace-en)

Try using your app with your eyes closed. On Android, switch on the TalkBack screen reader (Settings → Accessibility), or VoiceOver on an iPhone, and go through the app just by listening. Does the reader read the button as “Add expense”, or just “button”? Give every button and image a clear description. Make buttons at least 48 × 48 points; that’s what Android recommends so a finger can hit them comfortably. Then set the phone’s font size to maximum and see what breaks. **Criterion:** a tester with their eyes closed records an expense using only the reader, within one minute.
