**👥 Crew** · step 4 of 5 · [An appathon in one weekend](#expedice-aplikace-en)

You can enter your weekend app into a real competition. These three suit this world best:

- **[Global Appathon](https://www.appinventor.org/appathons):** a free online appathon from MIT App Inventor. In 2026, 2,176 people from 141 countries took part and built apps for the UN Sustainable Development Goals. You build in App Inventor, so it follows on from Saturday’s workshop. In English.
- **[Technovation Girls](https://technovationchallenge.org/program-overview/):** for girls aged 8 to 18, in the age groups 8–12, 13–15 and 16–18. A team has 1 to 5 people and an adult mentor, and over the season builds an app for a problem in their area. In English.
- **[Swift Student Challenge](https://developer.apple.com/swift-student-challenge/eligibility):** Apple’s competition for students. In Czechia from age 15, in Slovakia and Hungary from 16. You need an iPad or a Mac and the Swift Playgrounds app. The deadline is usually at the end of February.

You’ll find advice and ready-made solutions on the [App Inventor Community](https://community.appinventor.mit.edu) forum. You can read it without an account.

**Three rules for taking part safely:**
1. **Fill in the application with a parent or teacher.** Parental consent belongs to competitions for younger people; never get round it.
2. **Don’t publish real names, your school, your address or photos of children in the app, the video or your profile.** Make up the test data.
3. **Never send anyone passwords or codes, and don’t pay for anything.** All three competitions are free. Anyone who wants money for a “sure win” is a scammer.

> **Stay safe:** before you join a crew, read the page [Safe in communities](#bezpecne-v-komunitach-en). Set up your account with a parent and never give your address, school or phone number.
