**👀 Side trip** · [An appathon in one weekend](#expedice-aplikace-en)

Interface designers draw an app before the first block exists. Try [Penpot](https://penpot.app): it’s free, has open code and runs in the browser (set up the account with a parent). Draw three screens of your app and link them into a clickable prototype that people can try before you program anything. Your school may use [Figma for Education](https://www.figma.com/education/); on your own from 13, in the EU only from 16 in some countries. You’ll find the theory at [Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/) (in English): for example Fitts’s law (you hit a big, nearby button faster) or Hick’s law (the more options, the longer you take to decide). **Task:** find one place in your app that breaks Hick’s law, fix it, and use a stopwatch to measure how much faster the main task is now.
