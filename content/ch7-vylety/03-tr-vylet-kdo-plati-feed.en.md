The network is free. So who pays for it? Four steps and then back to the core of the book.

### Signals

A recommender learns from data, and that data is you. Every gesture you make is a **signal**, not just a like. The feed finds out that you liked a puppy video. But also that you watched a video to the end, that you skipped a cooking video after a second, that you stopped at a skateboarding video and opened the comments, and that you're still scrolling at eleven at night. Watch time is one of the strongest signals. You don't have to click on anything.

> **Back to the core:** A recommender [learns from data](#c/ai-se-uci-z-dat), from yours.

### A bubble in 20 rounds

The feed shows you more of what you click on. Then you click on that again, because that's what you see most. This is a **feedback loop**: the loop from chapter 2, reinforcing itself. A user who likes cats only a little more than other things has, after 20 rounds of strong reinforcement, a feed made largely of cats. Without reinforcement the feed stays varied.

> **Back to the core:** It's a [loop](#c/opakovani) that reinforces itself.

### Attention for sale

The network is free because **advertisers** pay for it. They pay so that their ad is seen by exactly the people they want. When the network knows nothing, it shows “New shampoo”. When it knows you are 12 and watch skateboarding videos to the end, it shows “A beginner's skateboard, ideal for your age”. When it knows your town too, “The skatepark in your town opens on Saturday”. The network isn't selling to you, it's selling you: your attention and data about you.

> **Back to the core:** Your [digital footprint](#c/digitalni-stopa) is worth something.

### My rules

Infinite scroll, autoplay and notifications are designed so that you don't stop. Only you can set the end of this loop. Pick at least three rules: I'll switch off autoplay of the next video, I'll switch off notifications for the apps that hold me up most, I'll set a daily time limit, I'll keep my phone out of my room at night, now and then I'll start watching something completely new so the bubble tears open.

> **Back to the core:** What you do about it is up to you: [you decide](#c/rozhodujes-ty).
