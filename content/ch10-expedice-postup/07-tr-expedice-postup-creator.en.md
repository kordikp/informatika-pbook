**🛠️ Side trip** · [Twelve December puzzles](#expedice-postup-en)

The **USACO Guide** is a free, open textbook of competitive programming written by students. It starts at the **Bronze** level: reading input, complete search of all possibilities, simulation and sorting. Every topic comes with problem sets and solutions. It is in English and suits you once you know the basics of Python or C++. Method: go through the General section, pick one topic a week, and for each problem write the tests first, and only then the code. It is a natural step towards olympiads and correspondence seminars.
