**👀 Side trip** · [Twelve December puzzles](#expedice-postup-en)

A **sorting network** sorts numbers by comparing several pairs **at the same time**. Following CS Unplugged, you draw it in chalk on the playground: people holding numbers walk along the lines and compare in pairs; the smaller goes left, the larger goes right. Design a network for **4 people** with as few comparisons as possible. Solution: 5 comparisons in three rounds, (1, 2) and (3, 4), then (1, 3) and (2, 4), and finally (2, 3). How do you check that it works? There are 24 orders of four numbers. It is smarter to try all **16 inputs made only of zeros and ones**. If the network sorts every such input, it sorts any numbers. In Vienna, students built a network for 50 people.
