**👥 Crew** · step 4 of 5 · [Twelve December puzzles](#expedice-postup-en)

**Advent of Code** is a yearly advent calendar of programming puzzles. Each day has a story, a small example and a big input. A puzzle has **two parts**: the second unlocks only after you solve the first, and it is often tricky, because a slow solution won’t do. You can use **any language**, even Scratch or pencil and paper. Since 2025 the event has 12 days and no global leaderboard, so it isn’t about speed. Past years stay open for solving all year.

**How to start:** you can only log in with an account from another service, such as GitHub or Reddit, and those are for ages 13 and up. If you are younger, ask a parent or the club leader, together with your crew, to use their account so you solve the puzzles together.

**How to do it as a crew:**
1. One puzzle a day, no more. Solve it **by hand on the small example** from the instructions first. That is also your first test.
2. Only then write a program and run it on the big input.
3. After solving, show each other your different methods and compare which is faster.

**Three rules for joining in safely:**
- **An adult handles the account.** Don’t get round the age limit and don’t set up an account without an adult.
- **No personal details.** Don’t put your name, school or town in your nickname. Don’t share photos or names of classmates without their consent.
- **Only read the discussions.** On forums just read, without an account. Don’t chat with strangers and don’t download anything from them.

**Success criterion:** at least three first parts solved and one second part, each with an example checked by hand.

> **Stay safe:** before you join a crew, read the page [Safe in communities](#bezpecne-v-komunitach-en). Set up your account with a parent and never give your address, school or phone number.
