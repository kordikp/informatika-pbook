**🚀 Project** · step 5 of 5 · [Twelve December puzzles](#expedice-postup-en)

Make up **three algorithmic puzzles of your own** in the style of Advent of Code: an easy one, a medium one and a hard one. Each puzzle has these parts:

1. **A story** from a life you know: a timetable, a tournament, a lift, the queue in the dining hall, a card collection.
2. **Exact instructions:** what the input is, what the output is and what the limits are. For example “a list of at most 1000 numbers from 1 to 100”.
3. **A small example** with an input and a result that the reader can check by hand.
4. **A second part** that changes the task so that a naive method is not enough. For example, instead of 10 cards there are a million.
5. **Tests in advance:** at least four, two of them edge cases (empty input, one item, the maximum, everything the same).
6. **A model solution** as pseudocode, a program or a decision tree. For the hard puzzle, **two methods** and a sentence on which is faster and why.

**Inspiration from this expedition:** weighing coins, the critical path, going through a graph, halving, sorting.

**Method:** break the puzzles down first. One hour for ideas, then for each puzzle separately the instructions, example, tests and solution. Try the solution on all the tests **before** you give the puzzle to anyone.

**Success criterion:** someone else solves at least one of your puzzles from the instructions alone, without asking questions. If they have to ask, the instructions are not yet unambiguous. Just like an algorithm from the core.
