**🔬 Side trip** · [Twelve December puzzles](#expedice-postup-en)

**Project Euler** is a collection of problems in which a clever method beats brute force. It is free, and you can read the problems without an account (in English). Start with problem 1: add up all the numbers below 1000 divisible by 3 or 5. First write an ordinary loop. Then try to calculate the answer **without a loop**, using only the formula for the sum of the series 3 + 6 + 9 + … Why do you have to subtract the multiples of 15? There are several hundred problems like this on the site, and the later ones a computer can’t solve by brute force even in a day. The site’s rule: solutions to problems beyond the first hundred are not shared. Set up an account with a parent.
