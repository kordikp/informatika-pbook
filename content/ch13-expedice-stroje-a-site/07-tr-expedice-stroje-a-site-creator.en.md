**🛠️ Side trip** · [Internet at the cabin](#expedice-stroje-a-site-en)

Once the extra game “How the cook calculates” hooks you, go to nandgame.com. Starting from a single NAND gate, you build all the other gates step by step, then an adder, memory and finally a processor. It’s free, in the browser and in English. Goal for the first week: an adder for four-bit numbers. Then try working out how many NAND gates your full adder has, and how many it would take to add two 64-bit numbers. If you want more, there’s a story version in the game Turing Complete on Steam (paid, through a parent’s account).
