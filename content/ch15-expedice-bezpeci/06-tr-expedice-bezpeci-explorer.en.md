**👀 Side trip** · [Operation Flag](#expedice-bezpeci-en)

Attacking tasks is fun, but real security work is mostly **defence**. Do a security audit of your household with your parents. Only with your parents’ consent, and change nothing without them.

**Method:**

1. **List the devices:** phones, computers, router, smart TV, camera, toys with Wi-Fi.
2. **For each one, find out:** does it have the default factory password? Does it get updates? When was the last one?
3. **Accounts:** does the family’s main e-mail have two-step verification? That’s because passwords to everything else are reset through e-mail.
4. **Backups:** where are the family photos? Do they follow the 3-2-1 rule (3 copies, 2 kinds of media, 1 away from home)?
5. **Passwords:** suggest a password manager to your parents, for example the free [Bitwarden](https://bitwarden.com/terms/) (account from age 13, younger kids as part of their parents’ family vault).

**Output:** a table “device, risk, measure, who will do it” and the three most important steps, ordered by how much protection they bring for how much work. This is exactly how security teams in companies work: first the map, then the priorities.
