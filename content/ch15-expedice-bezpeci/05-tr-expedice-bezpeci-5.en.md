**🚀 Project** · step 5 of 5 · [Operation Flag](#expedice-bezpeci-en)

Building a task that can be solved by one correct method and not by chance is harder than solving it. That’s why it’s the best test of understanding.

**Brief:** prepare a mini-CTF with **five flags**, one for each topic of the Safety and you chapter and one extra cipher flag:

1. **Password:** a puzzle in which, from clues (favourite animal, year of birth), you put together the weak password of a made-up character and work out why it falls straight away.
2. **Scam:** a made-up message with a tricky address. The flag is hidden in the real owner of the domain.
3. **Device:** a story about lost data. Only someone who recognises which backup survived finds the flag.
4. **Footprint:** a made-up profile (like Klára in the book) from which you can put together the flag, for example the name of an invented dog.
5. **Cipher:** a flag in layers that can be peeled off in CyberChef.

**Rules for making it:**

- Everything is **made up**. No real details of classmates, no real accounts, websites or domains.
- Every flag looks like `FLAG{…}`, so it’s clear when you’re done.
- For each task, write two hints and a solution for the teacher.
- **Test:** give each task to someone who didn’t see it being made. Where they get stuck at an unexpected place, the mistake is in the task, not in them.

**Format:** paper, a presentation, or a simple web page on GitHub Pages.
