**👀 Side trip** · [A hundred kilobytes](#expedice-weby-en)

Turn on the screen reader you already have: VoiceOver on iPhone and Mac, TalkBack on Android, Narrator on Windows. On the first day, learn three gestures or keys: next element, next heading, activate. Then every day, try to complete one task with your eyes closed: find the timetable on your school’s website, read an article on Wikipedia, go through your own website.

Write down what slowed you down the most. It will almost always be a “here” link, an image without a description or a button the screen reader doesn’t recognise. Fix it on your website and try again.
