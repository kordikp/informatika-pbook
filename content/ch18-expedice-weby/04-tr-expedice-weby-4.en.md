**👥 Crew** · step 4 of 5 · [A hundred kilobytes](#expedice-weby-en)

**Where you can join:**
- **js13kGames**: every year from August to September. You build a web game on an announced theme, and once zipped, the whole thing must be 13 kB at most. Your 100 kB budget is training. Free, in English, sort out the entry with a parent.
- **Gamedev.js Jam**: spring, web games on itch.io, free, in English. First play the games from last year’s jam and open F12 on them.
- **Hack Club**: a community and programmes for people aged 13 to 18. It rewards finished projects, such as a personal website. Free, in English. Younger kids read the projects without an account for now.
- **Junior Internet AMAVET** in Slovakia: a competition for young creators of websites and digital projects with a final in Bratislava, in Slovak.
- At home: show your website at your computing club or put it in the class gallery.

**Three rules for joining safely:**
1. **A nickname, not you.** No name, school, address or photo of your face: not on the website, not in your profile, not in the code.
2. **Never passwords or money.** Don’t send anyone a password or an SMS code, and don’t pay for anything. Your parents know where you join and where you have an account. Where the age limit is 13, younger kids go through a parent’s account, or just read.
3. **Show weird messages.** If someone writes to you privately, asks for photos or pushes you into something you don’t want, don’t reply. Show it to an adult and block them.

> **Stay safe:** before you join a crew, read the page [Safe in communities](#bezpecne-v-komunitach-en). Set up your account with a parent and never give your address, school or phone number.
