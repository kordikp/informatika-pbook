**🛠 Workshop** · step 1 of 5 · [A hundred kilobytes](#expedice-weby-en)

**Step 1: the skeleton (10 minutes).** Create a folder `muj-web` with a file `index.html` in it. Notepad is enough. Write:

```
<!doctype html>
<html lang="en">
<head>
  <meta charset="utf-8">
  <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1">
  <title>My website</title>
</head>
<body>
  <h1>Hi, Kometa here</h1>
  <p>I’m building a website under 100 kilobytes.</p>
</body>
</html>
```

Every line of the head has a reason: `lang` tells the screen reader to read in English, `charset` saves special letters and accents, `viewport` makes sure the page doesn’t shrink to an unreadable postage stamp on a phone.

**Step 2: check (5 minutes).** Open the file in a browser. Is the heading shown correctly?

**Step 3: a home for your website (15 minutes).** Choose one way:
- **Neocities**, with a parent: set up an account with your parent’s e-mail and choose a nickname. You upload the file by dragging it, and the website gets the address `prezdivka.neocities.org`.
- **GitHub Pages**, from age 13: a repository `prezdivka.github.io`, upload index.html, done.

Both hosts are free and give you https automatically.

**Step 4: the world test.** Turn off Wi-Fi on your phone and open the address over mobile data. If the website loads, the whole world can see it.

Rule for the whole expedition: the website shows a nickname, not your name, school or a photo of your face.
