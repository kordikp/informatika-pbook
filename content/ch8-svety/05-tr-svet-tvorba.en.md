You type a sentence, and a few seconds later you have a picture, a song or a video. The question is no longer “can you do it?” but “do you know what to do with it?”.

Eight core ideas of the book, told in the world of creating with AI. They come in the order in which a video or a poster is made: from visual language through files and the machines AI runs on to who will see the result and whose it is:

1. [An icon: a picture that leaves out almost everything](#svet-tvorba-abstrakce-a-model-en) · core *Model: a useful simplification*
2. [Why a minute of video takes hundreds of megabytes](#svet-tvorba-kodovani-en) · core *Everything is numbers*
3. [Why generating takes time](#svet-tvorba-uvnitr-zarizeni-en) · core *Inside a device*
4. [Where your prompt goes](#svet-tvorba-cloud-a-server-en) · core *The cloud is someone else's computer*
5. [Why a scientist is always an old man in a lab coat](#svet-tvorba-ai-se-uci-z-dat-en) · core *AI learns from examples*
6. [Six fingers and lettering from another planet](#svet-tvorba-generativni-model-en) · core *Generative AI predicts*
7. [Who will see your video](#svet-tvorba-doporucovac-en) · core *The recommender and your feed*
8. [Whose work is it](#svet-tvorba-rozhodujes-ty-en) · core *Check it, and you decide*

**Where to start creating:** ChatGPT, Gemini or Canva will make you a picture from a description, Suno will make music. You can edit a video on your phone in CapCut or in any other editor. Most tools have a free version, often from age 13 or with your parents’ consent, so read the terms.

**Creating is no longer only for people who can draw.** An illustration for a school report, a poster for your club, music for a video: ten ideas in an evening. But AI won’t pick the good one for you, won’t check whether it shows the truth, and won’t decide whether it is fair to publish it. That stays with you, and that is what this whole world is about.

**The world’s mission:** [Make a video with AI](#mission-svet-tvorba). You go through all eight tellings and design a video for the school open day. The mission counts toward the Solver certificate.
