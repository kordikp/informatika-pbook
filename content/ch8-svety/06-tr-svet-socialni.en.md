![The attention economy: who pays for a free network](images/diagram-ekonomika-pozornosti.en.svg)

**Why the network is free:** because it doesn’t sell the app to you, it sells your attention to advertisers. The longer you watch, the more ads you see, and the more precisely the network knows what to offer you. None of this is a secret conspiracy. It is a business model, and once you know it, you can make your own decisions.

**Age:** most of the big networks set a minimum age of 13 in their terms, and they often have stricter default privacy settings for younger users. Go through them together with someone you trust.

**The world’s mission:** [Take control of your feed](#mission-svet-socialni). You go through all ten tellings and write a guide to social media for someone younger. The mission counts toward the Solver certificate.
