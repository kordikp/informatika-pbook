The **micro:bit** is a school board smaller than a pack of chewing gum. It has a 5 × 5 LED display, two buttons, an accelerometer (it senses movement and shaking), a compass, a thermometer and a light sensor. Newer versions also have a microphone and a speaker. And a radio that micro:bits use to send each other messages. You program it in [MakeCode](https://makecode.microbit.org) from blocks, as in Scratch, or in Python. No board? MakeCode has a **simulator** right in the browser: a virtual micro:bit that presses its own buttons and even shakes itself.
