Every app on your phone started as an idea on paper. Here you see what is underneath: the method, the states, the data, the server and what the app is allowed to do.

Eleven core ideas of the book, told in the world of apps. They come in the order in which an app is made: from the method and breaking it into parts, through states and data, to the server, permissions and code from AI:

1. [Who owes whom how much](#svet-aplikace-algoritmus-en) · core *Algorithm: exact instructions*
2. [An app on paper](#svet-aplikace-dekompozice-en) · core *Break the problem down*
3. [A traffic light with a hidden bug](#svet-aplikace-over-a-oprav-en) · core *Test and fix*
4. [The same button, three reactions](#svet-aplikace-promenna-en) · core *Variable: a program's memory*
5. [An app made from a spreadsheet](#svet-aplikace-tabulka-a-evidence-en) · core *Table: data in order*
6. [Who may do what in the class app](#svet-aplikace-informacni-system-en) · core *Information system: who may do what*
7. [The app says you walk less](#svet-aplikace-z-dat-informace-en) · core *From data to information*
8. [The app crashed and took the text with it](#svet-aplikace-uvnitr-zarizeni-en) · core *Inside a device*
9. [Frontend and backend](#svet-aplikace-cloud-a-server-en) · core *The cloud is someone else's computer*
10. [An app from AI: who answers for it](#svet-aplikace-rozhodujes-ty-en) · core *Check it, and you decide*
11. [The torch that wanted your contacts](#svet-aplikace-ochrana-zarizeni-en) · core *Protecting your devices*

**Where to start building:** without a single line of code. In [App Inventor](https://appinventor.mit.edu) you put a mobile app together from blocks, like in Scratch. Tools like Glide turn an ordinary spreadsheet into an app. When you hit the limits of the blocks, code comes next, your own or from AI. The thinking stays the same: which screens, which data, what happens after a tap and who is allowed to do what.

**No-code or code?** A standard task, such as a register, a form or a catalogue, is quickest to put together from blocks. As soon as you want something the makers of the platform didn’t think of, the block simply doesn’t exist. And an app made of blocks lives on someone else’s platform: when it closes down or gets more expensive, you have a problem.

**The world’s mission:** [Build an app](#mission-svet-aplikace). You go through all eleven tellings and design an app for your class. The mission counts towards the Solver certificate.
