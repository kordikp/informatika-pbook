**🛠 Workshop** · step 2 of 5 · [The fact-checking team](#expedice-socialni-en)

**Tool:** the InVID-WeVerify plugin (in the browser store it is called “Fake news debunker by InVID & WeVerify”). It is developed by AFP Medialab for journalists, it is free and it works in Chrome and Firefox. The interface is in English.

**The first 30 minutes, step by step:**

1. **(5 min) Installing.** Install the extension with a parent and only from the browser’s official store. It doesn’t need an account or any personal details.
2. **(10 min) Keyframes.** Open the *Keyframes* tab and paste a link to a public YouTube video, for example a famous viral animal video. The plugin picks the frames where the scene changes.
3. **(10 min) Reverse search.** For two frames, click to search in several search engines at once. Look for the **earliest** appearance. Who uploaded the video first? Does the date match the caption?
4. **(5 min) Photo metadata.** Take a photo of your bedroom window with your phone (nobody in the photo) and open it in the *Metadata* tab. You will find the date, the phone model, maybe even the GPS location. Then send the same photo to yourself through a chat app, download it and check again. Has the location disappeared?

**What to write down:** one video, its earliest appearance and one thing the photo gave away.

**Rules:** only look into public videos and your own photos. Don’t upload other people’s private photos to tools. The plugin is not a magic wand: it gives you clues, the decision is yours.
