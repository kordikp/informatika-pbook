**🚀 Project** · step 5 of 5 · [The fact-checking team](#expedice-socialni-en)

**Task:** design the rules of a feed for a twelve-year-old girl and test them on paper or in a spreadsheet.

**What your design must include:**

1. **Signals and weights.** Pick 3 to 5 signals (watching to the end, like, share, “Not interested”, maybe even “sent it to a friend”) and give each one a weight. For each weight, say why in one sentence.
2. **Exploration.** What percentage of the feed will be a new topic, and how it is chosen.
3. **The end.** A stopping condition: after how many videos or minutes does the feed say “That’s all for today”? Where in the loop do you put it?
4. **A safeguard.** One rule that watches something other than attention: for example, that one author isn’t repeated, or that a video suspected of being a lie (like the hail from step 1) doesn’t spread before someone checks it.

**Test:** make up a table with five topics (or use the one from the challenge) and work out what your feed would show in the first 10 videos. Then tap “Not interested” three times on the winning topic and work it out again. If the feed hasn’t changed, the weights don’t work.

**Success criterion:** after 10 videos the feed contains at least 3 different topics, it stops according to your condition, and the order visibly changes after “Not interested”.

**Bonus:** film and edit a 60-second video “How my feed works” in Shotcut or Kdenlive. No classmates’ faces and no personal details, just hands, paper and numbers. Or write up your feed as your own telling in the author studio.
