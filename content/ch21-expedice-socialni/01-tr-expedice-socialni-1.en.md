**🧩 Challenge** · step 1 of 5 · [The fact-checking team](#expedice-socialni-en)

In half an hour, Ondra and Ema collect five clues. Your task: decide which clues **prove** something and which are **useless**, and write a verdict in one sentence.

- **A.** You put a keyframe from the video into reverse image search. The same shot turns up on an Italian photographer’s profile, uploaded on 14 June 2023.
- **B.** On a damaged car you can see a number plate with a blue strip and the letter I.
- **C.** The weather archive says that yesterday in Brno it was clear and 8 °C.
- **D.** The video has 2 million views and even Ema’s aunt shared it.
- **E.** Under the video, one comment says “I live in Brno, nothing like that” and another “Terrible, here too!”.

Then answer a harder question: **is it a deepfake?** And what does that mean for sharing?

**Success criterion:** correctly sorted clues and a verdict that says what in the video is true and what is a lie.

:::reveal 🔑 Solution
A, B and C prove something. A shows an older, different origin, B shows an Italian car (I is the international code for Italy), C shows that there could not have been hail in Brno. D and E prove nothing: the number of views measures popularity, not truth, and the comments contradict each other and nobody can check them. Verdict: **the video is real, but old and from Italy. The caption lies, not the picture.** It is not a deepfake. Most lies on social media look like this: a real shot in a fake context. The best reaction is not to delete it and stay quiet, but to write in the group where the video really comes from and add a link to the source.
:::
