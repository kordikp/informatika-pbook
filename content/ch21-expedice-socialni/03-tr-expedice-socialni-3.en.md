**👥 Crew** · step 3 of 5 · [The fact-checking team](#expedice-socialni-en)

**Who they are:** Wikimedia Czech Republic runs Wikiclubs (Prague, Plzeň, České Budějovice) and edit-a-thons, meetings where people write and fix articles together. It is free and in Czech. There is no age limit, but go to your first meeting with a parent.

**How to join:**

1. With a parent, find the nearest Wikiclub or edit-a-thon in the Wikimedia ČR calendar.
2. Create an account with a nickname. Careful: if you edit without an account, your IP address is shown with the edit.
3. Start with a small fix: a missing source, a typo, an out-of-date number. Back up every change with a link to a reliable source.
4. On paper, write out how a sentence with a source could be added to an article based on your clues from step 1. Wikipedia doesn’t want your opinion, it wants a fact that can be checked.

**Three rules for joining in safely:**

- **A nickname with no personal details.** No full name, school, age or town in your username or on your user page.
- **Talk in public.** Talk to others on the articles’ talk pages, not in private messages with strangers. If someone pushes you into a private conversation outside Wikipedia, ignore them and tell an adult.
- **Don’t sort out arguments without an adult.** If someone reverts your edit or is unpleasant, don’t argue. Ask at the Wikiclub or on the talk page what was wrong.

**If you would rather just read:** open the *History* tab on any article and follow who changed what and why. That is fact-checking too.

> **Stay safe:** before you join a crew, read the page [Safe in communities](#bezpecne-v-komunitach-en). Set up your account with a parent and never give your address, school or phone number.
