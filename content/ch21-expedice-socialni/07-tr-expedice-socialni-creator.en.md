**🛠️ Side trip** · [The fact-checking team](#expedice-socialni-en)

Paper is behind you, now you can build the feed for real. On Bluesky, anyone can create their own feed and offer it to others. The easiest way is the visual builder **SkyFeed**: you put blocks together like in Scratch. Input (all posts or posts from chosen authors), filter (keyword, language, images) and sorting (by time or by likes).

**How:** Bluesky requires an account from age 13, so younger kids work with a parent through the parent’s account. Build a feed about your hobby, for example astronomy. First sort it by time, then by likes. Write down three differences. Which feed is more interesting, and which is fairer to new authors?

**For programmers:** the starter kit *bluesky-social/feed-generator* is in TypeScript. Try rewriting the example feed *whats-alf* so that it picks your topic and, in one out of every five positions, a random post: that is your exploration from the project.

**Rules:** the feed must not collect data about specific people, and don’t share it publicly without a parent’s permission.
