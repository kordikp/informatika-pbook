**👀 Side trip** · [The fact-checking team](#expedice-socialni-en)

Lots of articles about monuments near you have no photo. Every autumn there is a worldwide competition, **Wiki Loves Monuments**: people photograph monuments and upload them to Wikimedia Commons, where anyone in the world can use them. Check on the competition page whether your country is taking part this year. Outside the competition you can upload all year round, and Wikimedia ČR also runs its own competition, Czech Wiki Photo.

**Challenge for fact-checkers:** find a monument near you that has an article but no photo. Photograph it from two angles and, with a parent, upload it with a caption, date and place. Then look at your photo’s metadata (the workshop from step 2): here the location is wanted, because it belongs to the monument, not to you.

**Rules:** no recognisable strangers or your own house may be in the photo. Photos from home have no location. Don’t write who took the photo in the description, except for your nickname.
