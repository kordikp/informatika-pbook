The digital creation club at the youth centre is planning a screening of short films at the end of the summer holidays and needs a twenty-second jingle. You will make it together with Ema. The club’s rule is: you may use AI, but the background must come from code, the mascot must be drawn by hand pixel by pixel, the music must come from your own notes, and the credits must say honestly who did what.

**🎯 Expedition goal:** Make a 20-second jingle: a generative background in p5.js, a pixel mascot in Piskel, chiptune in BeepBox and credits with the correct licences and an honest note about AI.

**Five steps:**

1. 🧩 Challenge: [The star that closes](#expedice-tvorba-1-en)
2. 🛠 Workshop: [A background from code in 30 minutes](#expedice-tvorba-2-en)
3. 🔍 Analysis: [A film you can take apart](#expedice-tvorba-3-en)
4. 👥 Crew: [Show it to the world, safely](#expedice-tvorba-4-en)
5. 🚀 Project: [The jingle](#expedice-tvorba-5-en)

**Extra side trips:** 👀 [Music: human versus machine](#expedice-tvorba-explorer-en) · 🛠️ [The mascot in 3D](#expedice-tvorba-creator-en) · 🔬 [Movement from code](#expedice-tvorba-thinker-en)

At the end there is a final test: you describe your plan in your own words and AI checks it against the points in the task. Reward: **Golden Clapperboard badge**.
