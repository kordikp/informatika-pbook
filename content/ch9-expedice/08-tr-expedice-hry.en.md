At six on Friday evening, a weekend game jam announces the theme “Everything speeds up”. You and Ema have 48 hours to turn an idea into a game that even a stranger can finish.

**🎯 Expedition goal:** Master fair randomness, a first game in Godot, joining a jam safely and analysing a real game, then finish your own game and plan the next one.

**Five steps:**

1. 🧩 Challenge: [⭐ Randomness that plays fair](#expedice-hry-1-en)
2. 🛠 Workshop: [🛠️ Your first Godot game in 30 minutes](#expedice-hry-2-en)
3. 👥 Crew: [🤝 A jam you can join](#expedice-hry-3-en)
4. 🔍 Analysis: [🔍 Analysis: how Celeste jumps](#expedice-hry-4-en)
5. 🚀 Project: [🎮 A game by Sunday evening](#expedice-hry-5-en)

**Extra side trips:** 👀 [A game for a tiny computer](#expedice-hry-explorer-en) · 🛠️ [An enemy that thinks](#expedice-hry-creator-en) · 🔬 [Fixed physics step](#expedice-hry-thinker-en)

At the end there is a final test: you describe your plan in your own words and AI checks it against the points in the task. Reward: **Golden Joystick**.
