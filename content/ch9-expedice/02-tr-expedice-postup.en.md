In December, Tomáš announces a challenge at the computing club: twelve evenings, one puzzle each evening. You join in with Ema. The winner is not the fastest, but whoever comes up with the cleverest method and can defend it.

**🎯 Expedition goal:** Go through five steps, from a hard problem through real tools and a competition to your own collection of algorithmic puzzles, and put together a plan for your own project.

**Five steps:**

1. 🧩 Challenge: [Evening one: the Towers of Hanoi](#expedice-postup-1-en)
2. 🛠 Workshop: [Weekend: the maze in Blockly Games](#expedice-postup-2-en)
3. 🔍 Analysis: [Analysis: two sorting algorithms in VisuAlgo](#expedice-postup-3-en)
4. 👥 Crew: [Crew: Advent of Code with the whole club](#expedice-postup-4-en)
5. 🚀 Project: [Your own collection of three puzzles](#expedice-postup-5-en)

**Extra side trips:** 👀 [No computer: a sorting network on the pavement](#expedice-postup-explorer-en) · 🛠️ [Competitive programming with the USACO Guide](#expedice-postup-creator-en) · 🔬 [Project Euler](#expedice-postup-thinker-en)

At the end there is a final test: you describe your plan in your own words and AI checks it against the points in the task. Reward: **A brass hourglass (badge of the Method expedition)**.

**[🎒 Set off on the expedition](#mission-expedice-postup)** · [🗺️ Signpost: tools, projects and communities](#expedice-postup-rozcestnik-en)
