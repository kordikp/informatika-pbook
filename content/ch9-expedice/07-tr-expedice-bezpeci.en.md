Ondra once had his game account stolen, and since then he’s been curious about how attackers think. During the autumn holidays you sit with him at the kitchen table over the first task of a CTF (Capture the Flag): a competition in which you look for a hidden text in each task, the so-called flag. By the end of the holidays you want to have your own mini-CTF ready for the whole class.

**🎯 Expedition goal:** Go through five steps from the first flag to your own mini-CTF: peel off layers of encoding, master a real tool, CyberChef, take apart a service that checks a password without seeing it, find a safe competition and build tasks for others.

**Five steps:**

1. 🧩 Challenge: [Three layers](#expedice-bezpeci-1-en)
2. 🛠 Workshop: [The CyberChef kitchen](#expedice-bezpeci-2-en)
3. 🔍 Analysis: [The password nobody sees](#expedice-bezpeci-3-en)
4. 👥 Crew: [Find your competition](#expedice-bezpeci-4-en)
5. 🚀 Project: [Build a CTF for 7.B](#expedice-bezpeci-5-en)

**Extra side trips:** 👀 [Home defender: a family security audit](#expedice-bezpeci-explorer-en) · 🛠️ [The terminal trail: OverTheWire Bandit](#expedice-bezpeci-creator-en) · 🔬 [The maths of ciphers: RSA in miniature and CryptoHack](#expedice-bezpeci-thinker-en)

At the end there is a final test: you describe your plan in your own words and AI checks it against the points in the task. Reward: **Silver Flag**.
