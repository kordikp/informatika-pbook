The computing club is holding a weekend mini-appathon in the library during the autumn half-term: two days, one app, no marks. Ema brings an idea, because her brother’s scout troop never knows who is bringing the tents and who paid for the bus. Tomáš knows the blocks in App Inventor, and your job is to make sure the app calculates correctly and can be used with a thumb, even in gloves.

**🎯 Expedition goal:** In one weekend, build a working prototype of an app for a real group of people, test it with five users and get it ready for a real appathon.

**Five steps:**

1. 🔍 Analysis: [Take apart an app that already does it](#expedice-aplikace-1-en)
2. 🛠 Workshop: [Your first app in 30 minutes](#expedice-aplikace-2-en)
3. 🧩 Challenge: [How many people are enough for a test](#expedice-aplikace-3-en)
4. 👥 Crew: [Find your appathon](#expedice-aplikace-4-en)
5. 🚀 Project: [Version 2 for real people](#expedice-aplikace-5-en)

**Extra side trips:** 👀 [Interface design](#expedice-aplikace-explorer-en) · 🛠️ [From blocks to code](#expedice-aplikace-creator-en) · 🔬 [An app for everyone](#expedice-aplikace-thinker-en)

At the end there is a final test: you describe your plan in your own words and AI checks it against the points in the task. Reward: **Golden Prototype badge**.
