Over the spring holidays the library is running an AI workshop, and Ema wants to train a model that recognises the birds at the feeder outside the window. Ondra says it can’t be done without a supercomputer. In five steps you’ll find out who is right, and build your own model that can honestly say where it makes mistakes.

**🎯 Expedition goal:** Train, test and honestly describe your own machine learning model, and plan a project with it.

**Five steps:**

1. 🧩 Challenge: [The model scores 83%. For whom?](#expedice-ai-1-en)
2. 🛠 Workshop: [Workshop: your first model in 30 minutes](#expedice-ai-2-en)
3. 🔍 Analysis: [Analysis: 50 million doodles](#expedice-ai-3-en)
4. 👥 Crew: [Crew: who to keep training with](#expedice-ai-4-en)
5. 🚀 Project: [Project: a model with a card](#expedice-ai-5-en)

**Extra side trips:** 👀 [A neural network for real](#expedice-ai-explorer-en) · 🛠️ [A fair model](#expedice-ai-creator-en) · 🔬 [Inside GPT-2](#expedice-ai-thinker-en)

At the end there is a final test: you describe your plan in your own words and AI checks it against the points in the task. Reward: **Crystal Neuron badge**.
