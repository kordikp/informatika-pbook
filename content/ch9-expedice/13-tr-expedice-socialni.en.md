It is Saturday and there is a wiki edit-a-thon at the library: people are improving Wikipedia articles together. Meanwhile, Ondra gets a video in the class group chat, “Yesterday in Brno: hailstones the size of tennis balls”, and half the school has already shared it. Ema suggests that you all become a fact-checking team for one weekend.

**🎯 Expedition goal:** Check a viral video the way professional fact-checkers do, learn a real tool for analysing videos, join the Wikipedia community, take apart a real feed algorithm and design your own fair feed.

**Five steps:**

1. 🧩 Challenge: [The hail that never fell](#expedice-socialni-1-en)
2. 🛠 Workshop: [An X-ray for video](#expedice-socialni-2-en)
3. 👥 Crew: [Wikiclub: a community that wants a source](#expedice-socialni-3-en)
4. 🔍 Analysis: [Under the bonnet of a real feed](#expedice-socialni-4-en)
5. 🚀 Project: [A fair feed](#expedice-socialni-5-en)

**Extra side trips:** 👀 [Photos Wikipedia is missing](#expedice-socialni-explorer-en) · 🛠️ [Your own feed for real](#expedice-socialni-creator-en) · 🔬 [Design a vaccine game](#expedice-socialni-thinker-en)

At the end there is a final test: you describe your plan in your own words and AI checks it against the points in the task. Reward: **Fact-checker’s magnifying glass (badge)**.
