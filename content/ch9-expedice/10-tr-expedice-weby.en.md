For the autumn half-term, Ema comes up with a bet: who can build the better personal website that is under 100 kilobytes in total, opens even on an old phone and can be read by a screen reader? You’re in. By the end of the holidays you both need an address you can send to anyone in the world.

**🎯 Expedition goal:** Build and publish your own website under 100 kB that is fast, accessible, works on a phone and doesn’t track its visitors.

**Five steps:**

1. 🛠 Workshop: [Your first address in 30 minutes](#expedice-weby-1-en)
2. 🧩 Challenge: [A 100-kilobyte budget](#expedice-weby-2-en)
3. 🔍 Analysis: [Analyse a website that has worked for twenty years](#expedice-weby-3-en)
4. 👥 Crew: [Where others build](#expedice-weby-4-en)
5. 🚀 Project: [Your website under 100 kB](#expedice-weby-5-en)

**Extra side trips:** 👀 [A week with a screen reader](#expedice-weby-explorer-en) · 🛠️ [Layout and dark mode](#expedice-weby-creator-en) · 🔬 [100 points in Lighthouse](#expedice-weby-thinker-en)

At the end there is a final test: you describe your plan in your own words and AI checks it against the points in the task. Reward: **The 100 kB Feather badge**.
