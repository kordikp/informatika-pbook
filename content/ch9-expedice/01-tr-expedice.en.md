The book showed you the cores. The expeditions take you further, to real tools, competitions and people who enjoy the same things as you.

An expedition is a journey over several evenings. Each one follows on from one chapter or one world of the book. It is harder than the rest of the book, you can take as much time as you like, and it does not count towards the certificate. You do it because the topic has hooked you.

Every expedition has **five steps**, each of a different kind. The order differs between expeditions, but the last one is always a project:

- 🧩 **Challenge**: a task that will make you sweat. Where it has a hint or a solution, they are hidden and you open them when you want.
- 🛠 **Workshop**: you try out a real tool that professionals work with too.
- 🔍 **Analysis**: you look at how a thing works inside and where it fails.
- 👥 **Crew**: you find a competition, club or community where you can carry on with others.
- 🚀 **Project**: you design something of your own.

On top of that there are three **side trips**: 👀 see it in action, 🛠️ try it with your own hands and 🔬 go deeper. At the end there is a **final test**: you describe your plan in your own words and AI checks it against the points in the task. If you pass, you get the expedition badge.

You will find shorter tasks for a single evening right in the chapters: every core has an **⭐ extra challenge** and many also have an **extra game**.
