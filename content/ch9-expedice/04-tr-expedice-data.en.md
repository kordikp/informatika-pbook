During the autumn half-term, Ema claims that the number 7 bus is late mainly when it rains. Tomáš says it’s only a feeling, because waiting in the rain is worse. You agree to let data settle the argument, and you turn the result into a poster for the international statistical poster competition.

**🎯 Expedition goal:** Go through the whole data cycle on your own question: design a table, collect and clean the data, analyse them in a real tool, draw an honest graph and write an explanation, including what is missing from the data.
