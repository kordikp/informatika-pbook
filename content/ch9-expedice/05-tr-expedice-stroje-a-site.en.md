During the spring holidays you go with Ondra to his grandad’s cabin, where the signal barely reaches. Ondra brings an old laptop, and you want to play over the network and have somewhere to save photos from your trips. You decide to build your own little internet: first in a simulator, then for real.

**🎯 Expedition goal:** Design and build in a simulator a network for the cabin with two subnets, a router, DNS and a web server, test it, and explain the route of one packet from start to finish.

**Five steps:**

1. 🧩 Challenge: [How many addresses fit into the cabin](#expedice-stroje-a-site-1-en)
2. 🛠 Workshop: [Workshop: your first network in Filius](#expedice-stroje-a-site-2-en)
3. 🔍 Analysis: [Analysis: how a packet crosses the ocean](#expedice-stroje-a-site-3-en)
4. 👥 Crew: [Crew: your code on the space station](#expedice-stroje-a-site-4-en)
5. 🚀 Project: [Project: the cabin network](#expedice-stroje-a-site-5-en)

**Extra side trips:** 👀 [Linux in the browser](#expedice-stroje-a-site-explorer-en) · 🛠️ [Build a computer from gates](#expedice-stroje-a-site-creator-en) · 🔬 [Networking like a pro](#expedice-stroje-a-site-thinker-en)

At the end there is a final test: you describe your plan in your own words and AI checks it against the points in the task. Reward: **Golden Router**.
