It’s December, and after coding club Tomáš shows you Advent of Code: from 1 December, one programming puzzle every day, twelve in all. For each part you solve, a star lights up. You agree that this year you’ll try it in real Python, not in blocks.

**🎯 Expedition goal:** Move from blocks to text code: write, run and debug your first programs in Python, read the history of a real project in Git, and invent your own puzzle for someone else to solve.

**Five steps:**

1. 🛠 Workshop: [Workshop: your first program in 30 minutes](#expedice-program-1-en)
2. 🧩 Challenge: [Challenge: the thermometer at the cabin](#expedice-program-2-en)
3. 🔍 Analysis: [Analysis: the history of a real project](#expedice-program-3-en)
4. 👥 Crew: [Crew: Advent of Code safely](#expedice-program-4-en)
5. 🚀 Project: [Project: your own puzzle](#expedice-program-5-en)

**Extra side trips:** 👀 [The same code in JavaScript](#expedice-program-explorer-en) · 🛠️ [Real Git on your computer](#expedice-program-creator-en) · 🔬 [Debugging as a detective story](#expedice-program-thinker-en)

At the end there is a final test: you describe your plan in your own words and AI checks it against the points in the task. Reward: **The golden Advent star**.
