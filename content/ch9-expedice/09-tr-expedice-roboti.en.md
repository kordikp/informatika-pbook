At an autumn weekend in the fablab, Ema brings a box: a micro:bit, two motors and a cardboard chassis. Ondra sticks a black line with a sharp bend and a crossroads onto wrapping paper. By spring you want a rover that drives along it without help, and then you want to take it to a competition.

**🎯 Expedition goal:** Design a rover in the simulator and then build it. It should follow the line, find it again even after a sharp bend and pass a set of tests. At the end, plan your own robot for a competition.

**Five steps:**

1. 🛠 Workshop: [A rover in 30 minutes in MakeCode](#expedice-roboti-1-en)
2. 🧩 Challenge: [The sharp bend and memory](#expedice-roboti-2-en)
3. 🔍 Analysis: [How a robot vacuum thinks](#expedice-roboti-3-en)
4. 👥 Crew: [Off to a competition with your crew](#expedice-roboti-4-en)
5. 🚀 Project: [A circuit you complete three times in a row](#expedice-roboti-5-en)

**Extra side trips:** 👀 [Arduino without soldering in Wokwi](#expedice-roboti-explorer-en) · 🛠️ [LEGO robots and Python with Pybricks](#expedice-roboti-creator-en) · 🔬 [Smooth driving instead of jerking](#expedice-roboti-thinker-en)

At the end there is a final test: you describe your plan in your own words and AI checks it against the points in the task. Reward: **The Golden Wheel**.
