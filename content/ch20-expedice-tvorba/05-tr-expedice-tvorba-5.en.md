**🚀 Project** · step 5 of 5 · [A jingle for film night](#expedice-tvorba-en)

**1. Mascot (Piskel).** Draw a 32 × 32 pixel mascot using only colours from the background palette. The animation has 4 frames, for example waving. Abstraction test: show it to a classmate for three seconds. Can they tell what it is?

**2. Music (BeepBox).** At a tempo of 120 beats per minute, 20 seconds is 40 beats, so **10 bars of 4 beats**. Write a melody, a bass line and drums. Let the last bar end with one long note. Export → WAV.

**3. Movement.** Save the best p5.js background as a picture, or try the side trip “Movement from code”.

**4. Editing.** In CapCut or another editor, put together the background, the mascot (GIF), the star logo and the music. Export the video in Full HD, 30 frames per second.

**5. Credits.** Who did what, with which tools, the licences of other people’s pieces and the sentence “With AI we…” (for example, came up with the name of the evening). If you don’t use AI, say that too.

**Success criteria:**
- length 20 seconds, give or take one second;
- background from code, mascot made pixel by pixel and music from your own notes;
- file size worked out in advance: at 8 Mb/s it is 20 × 8 : 8 = **20 MB**, and the real file matches;
- credits with the correct licences and an honest note about AI;
- the mascot is recognisable within three seconds.
