**👥 Crew** · step 4 of 5 · [A jingle for film night](#expedice-tvorba-en)

**Genuary** ([genuary.art](https://genuary.art)) is a generative art challenge: every January, 31 themes, one for each day. You may use any tool and you don’t have to follow the themes exactly. You will find the themes on [the prompts page](https://genuary.art/prompts), and you can create from them at any time of year. People share finished works on social media with the hashtag #genuary2026.

**Lospec Dailies** ([lospec.com/dailies](https://lospec.com/dailies)) is a daily theme for pixel art. The community meets on Discord, and that is for ages 13 and up.

**Your task:** choose three Genuary themes and make a sketch in p5.js for each (one of them can be the jingle background). Then draw one Lospec Dailies theme in Piskel.

**Three rules for joining in safely:**
1. **No personal details.** No name, school, town or face. A nickname and work that does not reveal where you live.
2. **Age limits apply.** Under 13 you only read without an account, or share with a parent through their account. Never change your age anywhere.
3. **Ignore private messages and invitations to go elsewhere.** If someone invites you to a private group, asks for a photo or is selling something, don’t reply and tell an adult.

And one extra rule: comments are about the work, not about you. Even the criticism “the colours clash” is a gift.

> **Stay safe:** before you join a crew, read the page [Safe in communities](#bezpecne-v-komunitach-en). Set up your account with a parent and never give your address, school or phone number.
