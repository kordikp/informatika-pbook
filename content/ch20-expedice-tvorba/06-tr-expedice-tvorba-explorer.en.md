**👀 Side trip** · [A jingle for film night](#expedice-tvorba-en)

Do a blind test. Three 20-second clips: your track from BeepBox, a melody from Song Maker and one by AI. If you are 13 and have your parent’s explicit consent, you can make the AI clip in Suno. Younger kids ask an adult to generate it, or do the test with just the two human clips.

**Task:** 1) Play the clips to five people in random order and ask: “Which one is by AI?” and “Which one do you like best?”. 2) Write the results in a table. 3) How many correct guesses would you get by pure chance with three clips? (One in three.) Did people recognise it better than chance? 4) Write three sentences: who the AI clip belongs to and what would need to be done to make it your track.
