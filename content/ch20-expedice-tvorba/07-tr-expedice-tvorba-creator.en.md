**🛠️ Side trip** · [A jingle for film night](#expedice-tvorba-en)

A pixel mascot is really a map of little blocks. In Tinkercad you can turn it into a 3D figure: each pixel is one 2 × 2 × 2 mm block. Under 13 you sign in to Tinkercad through a class set up by a teacher, or with a parent.

**Task:** 1) Work out how many blocks you will need (only coloured pixels, not transparent ones). 2) Build the mascot on a base 2 mm thick. 3) How many blocks do you save if you merge rows of the same colour into one box? That is the same trick as in video compression: don’t store repetition. 4) Export an STL and ask at the library or a fablab (a workshop with 3D printers open to the public) whether they will print it.
