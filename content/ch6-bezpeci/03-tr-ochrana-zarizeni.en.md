Your phone wants an update and you don't have time right now. Everything works, after all. Why does it keep insisting?

Every program has **bugs**, and attackers look for them. Harmful programs are called **malware**. They include the **Trojan horse**, a harmful program hidden inside something tempting, and **ransomware**, which encrypts your data and demands a ransom.

Your device is a castle. The **walls** are updates, the **gate** is app permissions, the **guard** lets in only trusted sources and the **second castle** is a backup somewhere else. For each attack in the mini-game, find the part of the castle that stops it.

One defence isn't enough. An antivirus doesn't catch everything, and phones can get viruses too. That's why there are **layers**. Updates patch the holes, trusted sources keep the Trojan horse out, permissions keep apps within limits and a **backup somewhere else** saves your data even if everything else fails.

A backup on the same disk isn't a backup. When the disk dies, it takes the backup with it.

**Try it!** Do a five-minute audit of your phone: ✅ automatic updates? ✅ all apps from the official store? ✅ photos backed up somewhere else? ✅ a screen lock? Whatever doesn't have a tick, fix it today.

> **Watch out, a common mistake:** “An antivirus protects me from everything.” An antivirus is just one guard. You also need updates, sensible app permissions and a backup in another place. And phones get viruses too.
