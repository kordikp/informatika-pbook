“Hi, I’m giving away free skins, just log in through this link.” It’s from a friend. Or from someone who has their account.

This attack doesn’t break the game or the computer. It breaks **you**. It plays on **reward** (a free skin), on **hurry** (today only!) and sometimes on **fear** (your account will be deleted). This is called **social engineering**. A fake page that looks like the game’s login is called **phishing**.

![A free-skins message and its warning signs](images/svet-hry-skiny.en.svg)

Scams used to give themselves away with spelling mistakes. Today **AI** writes the message without a single mistake, in your friend’s tone. In voice chat it can even imitate their **voice**. You can no longer spot a scam by its mistakes. You spot it by **what it wants**: a password, a code from a text message or money, and right now.

Defence has three steps:

1. **Stop.** Hurry is a sign of a scam, not a reason to rush.
2. **Check another way.** Message your friend somewhere else or call them: *did you send me this?* Log in to the game yourself through the official app, not through a link.
3. **Never share** your password or a two-factor authentication code. No game and no moderator will ever ask you for it.

**Try it!** Make up a message a scammer might send to a player of your favourite game. Then underline what it plays on: reward, hurry or fear?
