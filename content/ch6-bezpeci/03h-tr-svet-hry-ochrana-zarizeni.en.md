A cheat for unlimited money, free to download. You install it and you really do have the money. But it does more than that.

Every program has **bugs**, games included, and attackers look for them. When the maker finds a bug, they release a **patch**, in other words an **update**. That’s why a game updates even when it “worked”. A game or system that isn’t updated has a hole that attackers know about.

More dangerous is **malware** that you install yourself. A cheat or mod from an unknown website is often a **Trojan horse**: it does what it promised and quietly steals your Discord or game login at the same time. **Ransomware**, in turn, locks your files and demands a ransom.

How to protect yourself:

- **Download from trusted sources**: the official store, Steam, the page of a mod author with a good reputation. A link from a comment under a video is not trusted.
- **Watch the permissions.** Why would a torch game want your contacts? Why would a game mod need administrator rights on your computer?
- **Back up somewhere else.** Keep your saves, custom maps and creations in the game’s cloud or on another drive too. A copy somewhere else survives a virus, a lost phone and a broken drive.

**Try it!** Check on your phone which permissions your games have. Can you find one that wants something it doesn’t need for playing?
