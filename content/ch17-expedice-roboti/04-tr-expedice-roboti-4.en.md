**👥 Crew** · step 4 of 5 · [The rover that doesn’t fall off the table](#expedice-roboti-en)

Robotics is a team sport. In the Czech Republic and nearby, these competitions are waiting for you:

- **[Robosoutěž ČVUT](https://robosoutez.fel.cvut.cz)** for lower secondary pupils and the matching years at grammar schools. A team of friends builds and programs a robot from a LEGO kit for a set task. The organisers lend you the kit and you don’t need any previous experience. The spring rounds take place at FEL ČVUT in Prague. In 2026 the robots were putting together a “Tetris Puzzle”.
- **[FIRST LEGO League](https://www.first-lego-league.org/en/2026-27-season/bioglow)**: an international league of teams. The 2026/27 season has the theme BIOGLOW (ecosystems), and besides the robot the team also works on a research project. Tournaments run from November to April. The team is usually entered by a school or a club, and registration costs money.
- **[RoboCup Junior](https://junior.robocup.org/)**: an international competition with events such as line rescue or robot football. The rules are in English. In the Czech Republic there are regional competitions, for example Opavský ROBOCUP.

**How to start:** find 2 or 3 friends and one adult: a computing teacher, a club leader or a parent. Show them the rover and the table of tests. There’s no better proof that you’re serious.

**Three rules for taking part safely:**
1. An adult deals with the application and personal details. You don’t enter your address, phone number or date of birth anywhere.
2. Share photos and videos from training only with the consent of everyone in them, and without names and schools in the caption.
3. Team chats and groups are set up and run by an adult (Discord, for example, is only from age 13). You go to competitions with someone from the team, never to meet an unknown person from the internet.

> **Stay safe:** before you join a crew, read the page [Safe in communities](#bezpecne-v-komunitach-en). Set up your account with a parent and never give your address, school or phone number.
