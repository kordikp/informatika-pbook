**🚀 Project** · step 5 of 5 · [The rover that doesn’t fall off the table](#expedice-roboti-en)

Build your own circuit and turn the rover into a reliable machine.

**The track.** On wrapping paper or several A3 sheets taped together, stick black insulating tape 15 to 20 mm wide. Add a straight, a gentle bend, a sharp bend (90° or more) and a crossroads.

**Without hardware.** Try the same logic in [Open Roberta Lab](https://calliope.cc/en/coding/editors/open-roberta), where a virtual robot on wheels drives around. Or in MakeCode, click through the pins following a series of sensor values you wrote down in advance.

**Version log.** For every change, write three lines: *version, what you’re changing, what happened*. Always change just one thing: the speed, or a rule, or the pause. Otherwise you won’t know what helped.

**Measuring.** Time one lap with a stopwatch and count how many times you have to put the rover back on the line by hand.

**Success criteria:**
- the rover completes the circuit **3× in a row** without help;
- all five tests from the step “The sharp bend and memory” pass;
- you have at least three versions in the log and you know why the last one is better than the first.

**Presentation.** Film a 30-second video in which only your hands and the robot can be seen. Show the sharp bend and say in one sentence what the rover remembers.
