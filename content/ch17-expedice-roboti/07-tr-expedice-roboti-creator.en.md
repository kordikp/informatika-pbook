**🛠️ Side trip** · [The rover that doesn’t fall off the table](#expedice-roboti-en)

Does your school or club have LEGO SPIKE or Mindstorms? [Pybricks](https://pybricks.com) is open software that puts MicroPython onto the hub. You program from the browser on Windows, Mac, Linux, Chromebook and Android. Python is free, blocks are for a donation, and the interface is in English.

**Careful:** Pybricks changes the hub’s firmware. The hub belongs to the school, so talk to your teacher first. The original firmware can be put back, but the owner has to know about it.

**Task:** rewrite your line rules from MakeCode in Python. Almost every block has its own line: *forever* is `while True:`, *if* is `if`. On the page of [Pybricks projects for SPIKE Prime](https://pybricks.com/project/spike-prime/) you’ll find ready-made programs you can take apart. The same kits are used at Robosoutěž and in FIRST LEGO League, so you’re training for competitions straight away.
